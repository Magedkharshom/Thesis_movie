Storyline for the Results chapter (each sentence follows from the numbers above):
1. Static position in plot space adds at most 0.005 R² to year, genre, runtime and language (star power adds up to 0.059); the plot-network block is jointly significant for 3 of 4 outcomes (fig_01).
2. Plot measures with p < 0.05 after all controls (fig_02): betweenness → log revenue (+0.093); participation → log revenue (-0.065); clustering → log revenue (+0.068); betweenness → revenue percentile (+0.012); participation → revenue percentile (-0.008); clustering → revenue percentile (+0.007); betweenness → log popularity (+0.010); clustering → log popularity (+0.010); foresight → revenue percentile (+0.008); same-year competition → revenue percentile (-0.039); foresight → log ROI (+0.070); foresight → rating (+0.026); foresight → log popularity (+0.016).
3. Outcomes cluster on the plot network for 4 of 4 outcomes (Moran's I 0.053–0.121); the plot prior beats the genre prior out of sample for 4 of 4 outcomes (fig_03).
4. After controls, 14 of 35 labelled communities differ from the average film in rating, 11 in revenue percentile and 5 in both (fig_04).
5. 8 communities grew and 6 shrank significantly as a share of each year's films, 1990–2024 (fig_05).
All results are associations, not causal effects.
